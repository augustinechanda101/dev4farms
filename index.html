About DEV4 Farms

A Chisamba farm with a passion for rare and beautiful birds

DEV4 Farms breeds and supplies quality exotic birds — peacocks, ostriches, Sebastopol and Chinese geese, Pekin ducks, Silkie chickens and more. Every bird is raised with proper feed, clean water and daily care, so it arrives at your gate strong and healthy.

Whether you're starting an ornamental flock, adding show birds to your collection or venturing into ostrich farming, we're happy to share honest advice on housing, feeding and care.
