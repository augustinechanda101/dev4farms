About DEV4 Farms

A Chisamba-based farm with one simple goal: healthy, beautiful exotic birds and honest service to match.

Our story

From a small flock to a trusted exotic bird farm

DEV4 Farms began with a simple love for rare and beautiful birds. What started as a few ornamental chickens and ducks has grown into a dedicated exotic bird farm in Chisamba, Central Province — home to peacocks, ostriches, Sebastopol and Chinese geese, Pekin ducks, Silkies and more.

We breed and raise our own birds wherever possible, so we know exactly how each one has been fed, housed and handled. That means when you buy from us, you're getting a bird that's used to good care — not one that's been through half a dozen hands.

We supply smallholder farmers, homesteads, lodges, schools and collectors across Zambia, and we're always happy to share what we've learned about keeping exotic birds happy and healthy.

What we stand for

Four things we never compromise on.

Welfare first

Spacious housing, clean water, quality feed and daily handling. Healthy, calm birds are the only birds we sell.

Healthy stock

Strong breeding lines and careful brooding. Every bird is checked over before it leaves the farm.

Honest advice

We tell you exactly what a bird needs to thrive — housing, feed, companions — before you commit to buying.

Support after sale

Bought a bird from us? You can always call or WhatsApp with questions as it settles into your farm.

Come and visit the farm

We're located at Chikonkomene in Chisamba, Central Province, Zambia. Farm visits are by appointment — call or WhatsApp ahead so we can show you around properly.
