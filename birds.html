Our Birds

Quality exotic birds raised on our farm in Chisamba. Availability changes with the season — message us to confirm what's on the ground right now.

Peacocks

Available

Indian Blue peafowl — the showstopper of any farm. Males grow the famous iridescent train, and the birds are calm and easy to keep once established. Chicks, growers and adult pairs available in season.

Seasonal

Sebastopol Geese

Rare breed

One of the most ornamental goose breeds in the world — long, soft, curly feathers make them unmistakable. Gentle in nature and highly sought after by collectors and show keepers.

Pekin Ducks

Available

The classic pure-white duck. Hardy, fast-growing and friendly, Pekins are excellent layers and suit both ornamental ponds and utility flocks. Ducklings and growers usually available.

Chinese Geese

Available

Elegant, upright and alert — Chinese geese are superb foragers, productive layers and the famous "watchdogs" of the poultry world, quick to sound the alarm at anything unusual.

Silkie Chickens

Available

Little clouds of feathers with a gentle nature. Silkies are famous brooders and natural mothers, making them perfect pets, show birds and even foster mothers for other poultry.
